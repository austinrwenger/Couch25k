Hazard Decision Lab

Ready for the Unexpected

Five realistic situations. Identify the safest first action and the evidence that best supports it.

Before You Begin

Good decisions follow a priority.

1. ProtectMove away from the hazard or enter the correct shelter.
2. Get helpNotify the appropriate adult, staff member, or emergency service.
3. CommunicateGive useful facts without delaying protective action.

You will make two decisions in each situation: what should happen first and which fact best supports that choice. Some answers may sound helpful, but select the best response.
