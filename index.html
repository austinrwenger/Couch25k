Quick controls

Designed for a large interactive classroom display. Tap the big controls or use a keyboard:

  • Space start or pause
  • ← and → move between intervals
  • F enter full screen
  • M mute or unmute cues
  • R reset the session

The plan uses a 5-minute warm-up walk and 5-minute cool-down walk. Run means an easy, sustainable pace—not a sprint. Take a rest day between sessions, adapt for individual needs, and stop if something does not feel right.
